Major features
* Store customers, items, orders and invoices
* Generate standing orders for customers
* Send invoices to quickbooks for customers to pay them, and so the bakery can run reports
* Secure login for bakery
* Recipies for items
* Calendar view for standing orders each day
 * Clicking on day shows orders for that day
 * Can print order sheets to put with each delivery

* Customer logins
